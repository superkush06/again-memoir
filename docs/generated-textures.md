# Inferred texture assets

The built-in imagegen tool generated these project assets on October 24, 2026 after the configured Gemini image API returned HTTP 429. These textures invent missing views; they are not additional photographs of the original events. The application retains the original source texture on visible front surfaces.

- `data/generated-textures/instagram-color-atlas.png` — color street memory, original fingerprint `c31172304442754df2059a495bde9ed0`.
- `data/generated-textures/instagram-monochrome-atlas.png` — monochrome room memory, original fingerprint `02b903fb94fe38cb6e3daf83de3ea3dd`.

Each atlas contains two equal horizontal panels: completed background, then imagined rear surfaces. `scripts/import-world-atlas.ts` imports them into the local reconstruction cache, preserving the original background outside the subject masks. The source atlases, derived textures, and memory archive are ignored by Git. Back up `data/` to preserve them.

Generated people can shift relative to the reference even when the prompt requests matching positions. When the detected subject counts agree, local segmentation of the rear panel guides a silhouette warp back onto the original subject masks, reducing background pixels leaking into hair and clothes. Ambiguous counts retain the generated panel rather than risking a mismatched person. The color atlas uses alignment; the monochrome atlas retains its original generated rear panel. The original front texture is retained in both cases.

## Color atlas prompt

Use case: precise-object-edit. Asset type: photorealistic RGB texture atlas for a walkable reconstructed 3D memory. Input image is the edit target and exact source camera framing/color reference. Output a single wide image with TWO EQUAL-WIDTH panels, edge to edge with NO divider/borders/text/margins. Total aspect ratio 2.6:1, each half must match the entire source image 1.3:1 framing. LEFT HALF: the exact same nighttime street scene with all three foreground people completely removed; plausibly fill the street and storefront background their bodies occluded, continuing foliage overhead and pavement below. Keep the streetlight, distant signs, trees and original color/exposure in the same relative positions. RIGHT HALF: the exact source composition but each of the three people is rotated 180 degrees in place to depict the opposite side of each body, for texturing their 3D rear surfaces. Maintain each person's head, shoulders, hips, width and crop at the same image coordinates as the original. Keep the pale pink polo, gray shirt, and black shirt as the same original colors, photographic fabric detail and original lighting. For the middle and right subjects show the backs of heads and shirts, no duplicated faces or front graphics. For the left subject who originally faces away infer a plausible opposite side consistent with the same pale polo without claiming factual facial likeness. Both panels MUST preserve the full uncropped source framing and original saturated colors; do not turn monochrome, do not zoom, do not add unrelated props. This is a deliberately inferred missing view, not a claim about factual unseen appearance.

## Monochrome atlas prompt

Use case: precise-object-edit. Create a photographic texture atlas for a true 3D reconstructed memory from this edit-target photo. ONE image with TWO exactly equal-width panels directly side-by-side, NO text/borders/dividers, total aspect ratio 2:1. EACH HALF must maintain the full entire original square photo composition, same object image coordinates, crop and exposure. LEFT HALF: remove the three dominant foreground friends plus the partly visible dark-jacket person at left, plausibly filling the hidden room and floor; preserve the original ceiling, columns, walls, windows, LED string lights, and other distant background. RIGHT HALF: same original photo layout, but rotate the three dominant people and left dark-jacket person 180 degrees in place, keeping exact same head, shoulder and bottom/body positions and silhouettes. Show the BACKS of their hair, necks and same shirts with realistic fabric; they still have arms around one another. The sports jersey can have its plausible back jersey details, middle dark t-shirt back plain, right t-shirt back plain. Do not paint faces onto backs. Keep all subjects full-source size and at the same image coordinates to allow texture projection on an existing mesh. Preserve monochrome because this particular original is black-and-white; do not invent color. No added props, no extra people, no cropping or zooming. The missing views are intentionally imagined from the reference, with real photographic material detail and consistent lighting.
